# Inkhaven 1.6.3 — The World Remembered

_A worldbuilding-polish release that finishes the World→books weave begun in
1.6.2 and gives the world a memory of its own past. The last subsystem — the
ConLang suite — is now bridged; every proposal kind commits through one shared
path from the CLI or the TUI; and a new chronicle reads the compiled history as
the world's growth over the ages. No new runtime crates._

## World → ConLang (WORLD-13)

- **`inkhaven realworld propose-language`** reads each culture's language profile
  (`SVO · fusional · tonal`) and naming sample and proposes a *language* per
  people, into the same queue as your Places and rulers. Accepting scaffolds a
  full language book in the ConLang suite — Meta, Phonology, Grammar, Dictionary,
  Sample texts — with a `world-profile` brief already written in, so the handoff
  the book always described as "the World × ConLang bridge" is now automatic. The
  making of the language stays yours.

## One shared committer — and a fix

- Every world proposal — Place, Mythology, Character, or language — now commits
  through a single dispatcher shared by the CLI (`realworld proposals accept`)
  and the TUI (`Ctrl+B W → P`). This **fixes a correctness bug**: accepting a
  non-Place proposal in the TUI used to create a broken Place regardless of kind.
- The TUI's `Ctrl+B W → C` compile now proposes **all four kinds** at once, and
  its proposal-queue clear is kind-scoped — a CLI-made Mythology/ruler/language
  proposal is no longer wiped by a subsequent compile.

## A chronicle of the past (WORLD-13)

- **`inkhaven realworld chronicle`** reads the same compiled history as
  `realworld history`, but reports it as a *state trajectory*: at the close of
  each epoch, how far the world had grown — settlements by class, settled
  population, and realms standing — beside that epoch's events. You watch the
  Founding Age hold a handful of towns and the Present Age settle into the world
  your story opens in, realms having risen and waned along the way. It is pure
  presentation of the deterministic history — no simulation, no invented growth.

## The world at the desk, deeper

- **`Ctrl+B W` no longer freezes the UI.** The overview compiled the whole world
  chain on the UI thread (a stall on large / DEM worlds); it now compiles on a
  background thread, with an mtime-keyed cache so an unchanged world re-opens
  instantly.
- **The scene brief names your neighbours.** `realworld scene` now reports the
  nearest coordinate-bearing Place — distance and compass bearing — so a place
  you positioned by hand (`realworld set-coords`) grounds the scenes around it.

## Building the World with Inkhaven — the book

- New section **"The past as a rising tide"** (ch. 8) for the chronicle; ch. 10's
  ConLang-bridge section now covers `propose-language`; ch. 15's scene brief
  covers the nearest-neighbour line; plus command-reference rows.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** New commands: `realworld propose-language`,
`realworld chronicle`. The world proposal queue commits every kind through one
shared path (CLI + TUI). All additive — existing projects are unaffected.
