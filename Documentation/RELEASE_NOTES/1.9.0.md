# Inkhaven 1.9.0 — The Interactive Worldbuilder

_The 1.9 major (RFC WBLD-1): a full-screen companion for building a world by asking, not by
hand-editing HJSON. `inkhaven worldbuilder` keeps the world, its plausibility score, its facts,
its map, and its magic ledger all in front of you at once — and every change lands in the same
`world.hjson` the `realworld` compiler has always read. It never generates prose; the author
decides, and the worldbuilder measures, validates, and records._

## `inkhaven worldbuilder`

A third `TuiHost` sibling beside `inkhaven research` and the linguistic workbench. Four regions:
a **Facts** tree over a **World** tree down the left; a wide right pane that cycles
`Chat | Research | Map | Ledger` with `Ctrl+R`; a full-width **Query** prompt; and a status bar
carrying the world name and its live plausibility score. Launch with
`inkhaven worldbuilder [--session <name>] [--interview] [--from-map]`.

### Build by asking — the interview

`/interview` (or `--interview`) walks you through five stages — Sky, Land, People, Rules, and a
closing Review — one plain question at a time. You answer in your own words; each answer becomes
a **pending edit** and the score moves as you go, so the world firms up before a single line is
written to disk. Blank skips a question, `Esc` leaves without losing progress.

### Shape by command — the pending delta

`/set`, `/star`, `/tilt`, `/moon`, `/nation`, `/magic on|off`, and `/rule` each propose a
schema-valid edit to `world.hjson`, previewed before it joins the **pending delta**. `/diff`
lists it, `/undo` drops the last, `/reset` clears it, `/write` folds all of it in atomically —
and the pending delta is saved with your session, so uncommitted edits survive a quit.

### See what your choices imply

- **`/compile`** runs the whole deterministic chain (astronomy → geology → climate → hydrology →
  demographics) over the world as it stands and reports the compiled state — real year length,
  sea coverage, mean climate, rivers, population. From then on the worldbuilder reasons over the
  *simulated* world, not merely what you declared.
- **`/validate`** runs the plausibility lints and spells out the score with every warning graded
  high / medium / low — the same **plausibility score** (`★ NN`, with `▲`/`▼` deltas) that rides
  in the status bar and moves the instant an edit is accepted.
- **The Map pane** draws an ASCII biome minimap straight from the compiled layers — sea, forest,
  desert, ice, with rivers and settlements stamped over it. No external tool; works on any
  terminal.

### Record what is true

- **`/wfact <statement>`** records an author-decided fact into the Facts book, tagged
  `fact:world` (`◎`), and feeds it back into the worldbuilder's own context.
- **`/research <query>`** retrieves related Facts into the Research pane.
- **The Ledger pane** shows the world's declared magic exceptions and lints them live; `/rule`
  and `/magic` author them over the same delta engine.

### Keep the record

- **`/journey`** prints the session timeline — every accepted edit, write, and fact, each with
  its plausibility arc. **`/sessions`** lists named sessions.
- **`/export`** assembles a readable Markdown dossier — compiled state, plausibility report,
  magic ledger, recorded facts, and the whole journey — under `exports/`.

## Compatibility

No new dependencies. Every worldbuilder change is an ordinary `world.hjson` edit — nothing here
changes the `realworld` compiler, the fact-checker, or any existing project. Compiles
warning-free. Test suite → 2712.
