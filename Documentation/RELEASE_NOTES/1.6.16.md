# Inkhaven 1.6.16 — Sources & Contradiction

_Public-domain sources in, and the first tools that make them argue: an arXiv/preprint
bundle, two keyless public-domain adapters, and the opening slices of SCHOLAR — a
relation engine that reports where a claim is opposed **and** where it's confirmed.
No new runtime crates._

## The arXiv / preprint bundle

`inkhaven export tex --bundle <dir|zip>` writes a self-contained LaTeX submission — the
`.tex`, a `sources.bib` harvested from the Sources book, every referenced figure (copied
in, paths flattened), and a `MANIFEST.txt`. Because arXiv compiles the source on its own
machines, the bundle carries everything the build needs, and two `tylax` quirks are
corrected on the way out: a bibliography citation (`@key`, which tylax would emit as a
broken `\ref`) is rewritten to `\cite{key}` for the keys in `sources.bib` — leaving real
cross-references as `\ref` — and the bibliography style is mapped to a real `.bst`. Any
figure the prose names but that isn't on disk is listed in the MANIFEST, never silently
dropped. Composes with `--blind`.

## Public-domain source adapters

Two keyless adapters, both scoped to public-domain material and cloning the `/gutenberg`
pattern (fetch → chunk → embed as a `research_source`, auto-cited):

- **`/archive <query>`** — the **Internet Archive** (`advancedsearch.php`, `mediatype:texts`
  + OCR plain text). A vast public-domain reach across traditions and languages.
- **`/wikisource <query>`** — **Wikisource** via the MediaWiki API, in the **book's
  language** (`{lang}.wikisource.org`), so a Russian project reaches `ru.wikisource`
  natively (the Synodal Bible, Russian classics), a French one `fr.wikisource`, and so on.

Both have headless twins (`inkhaven research --archive` / `--wikisource`).

## SCHOLAR — the relation engine (contradiction *and* confirmation)

The first slices of a tool that relates two bodies — your **Text** (manuscript) and your
**Research** (Facts + ingested sources) — reporting both directions of the relation:

- **`/contradict`** (and `inkhaven research --contradict`) — a **structured, source-attributed**
  contradiction scan of the Facts. Where `/factcheck` emitted `<a> ⇄ <b>` text it never
  parsed, this parses each pair into an addressable finding joined to each fact's
  provenance, and splits **cross-source** (two sources disagree) from **within-source** (a
  source contradicting itself).
- **`/relate <claim>`** — retrieves the nearest facts **and** ingested-source passages, then
  grades each `CONTRADICTS | TENSION | QUALIFIES | AGREES | SILENT`, splitting **⚠ against
  your claim** (risks to address) from **✓ supporting your claim** (material to cite).

New modules `src/research/{contradiction,archive,wikisource}.rs`, `src/export/bundle.rs`.
Design docs: [`THEOLOGY_PHILOSOPHY_RFC.md`](../PROPOSALS/THEOLOGY_PHILOSOPHY_RFC.md),
[`SCHOLAR_PLAN.md`](../PROPOSALS/SCHOLAR_PLAN.md).

## Multilingual & Unicode-hardened

The adapters target the book's language, and the SCHOLAR prompts localize their reasoning
into it (en/ru/fr/de/es first-class). The contradiction/relation parsers were hardened to
accept **non-Latin numerals** (Arabic-Indic, Persian, Devanagari), so a model replying in
Arabic or Hindi doesn't silently drop findings. Verified end-to-end in Russian
(`/gutenberg` on a Russian project ingests the Cyrillic original, not the translation).

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** New commands `export tex --bundle`, `/archive`, `/wikisource`,
`/contradict`, `/relate` (+ `--archive` / `--wikisource` / `--contradict` CLI twins); new
config `research.archive` / `research.wikisource`. Everything is additive — existing
projects are unaffected. Test suite → 2414.
