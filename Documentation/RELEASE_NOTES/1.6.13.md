# Inkhaven 1.6.13 — Cite Your Claims

_A new check for nonfiction: every checkable claim should carry a source. The Sourcing
pass finds the sentences that make an assertion — a statistic, a date, a quotation, an
attributed finding — and cite nothing. No new runtime crates._

## The Sourcing pass (NF-CITE)

`inkhaven sources check` has always caught a `@key` you cite but never defined.
`inkhaven sources coverage` catches the opposite and more insidious slip: a sentence
that makes a **checkable factual claim and cites nothing at all**.

- **The fast track** is deterministic and zero-AI. It flags a sentence carrying no
  `@key` when it shows a high-precision claim signal — a **statistic** (`42%`,
  `3,400,000`), a **date** (a year), a **quotation** (six words or more), or an
  **attribution** ("according to…", "a study found…"). Ordinary prose and small numbers
  stay quiet. It exits non-zero when any uncited claim is found, so it drops into the
  same pre-publish / CI gate as `sources check`, `docs verify`, and `terms check`.
- **While drafting**, `Ctrl+V Shift+C` runs the same pass on the open paragraph and
  drops the flags into the Output pane — right beside the `Ctrl+V @` cite picker you use
  to fix them.
- Where a passage is genuine common knowledge, tag the paragraph **`no-cite`** and it
  stops asking.

### The AI track — tied to your research

`inkhaven sources coverage --ai` goes further. Per chapter it retrieves the relevant
entries from your **Facts book** and asks the model to find subtler uncited claims *and*
check each against your research — reporting them split into **"unsupported — research
or cite"** and **"backed by your Facts — just add the citation"** (naming the fact).
When your own notes already back a claim, it tells you so. (Needs a configured model;
degrades to plain claim-finding when there is no Facts book.)

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** New command `inkhaven sources coverage [--ai --provider
--book-name --json]`; new chord `Ctrl+V Shift+C`. Everything is additive — existing
projects are unaffected; the pass runs only when you ask for it. Test suite → 2364.
