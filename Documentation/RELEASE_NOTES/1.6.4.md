# Inkhaven 1.6.4 — The Whole World

_A worldbuilding gap-fill release. The human half of the world now persists in
the World book beside the physical layers; settlements can be named in each
realm's own phonic style; declared landmarks reach the map; the scene brief sees
more of its neighbourhood; and two long-deferred correctness bugs are fixed. No
new runtime crates._

## The human half, in the book (WORLD-14)

- `realworld compile --materialize` (and the TUI's `Ctrl+B W → C`) now writes the
  compiled **Nations**, **Cultures**, and **Ecology** into the World book, beside
  the astronomy / geology / climate / hydrology / demographics chapters. The whole
  world — physical and human — is now searchable and citable from your prose, not
  just the half the earlier releases persisted.

## Names in the world's own style (WORLD-14)

- **`inkhaven realworld name`** proposes a name for every settlement in its
  realm's phonic style: each realm draws a small onset/tail palette, so its towns
  share a family sound and differ from another realm's — a Karon coast of
  Torvelras and Velkaeths, a Serai valley that sounds nothing like it — instead of
  the generic placeholder names. Deterministic; a naming aid you adopt when you
  accept a Place, or supersede once you have realised the realm's tongue in the
  ConLang suite. (This fulfils a naming elaboration deferred since the first
  demographics work.)

## Declared landmarks on the map (WORLD-14)

- A `geography.landmarks` entry given a position — `lat`/`lon` degrees or raw grid
  `x`/`y` — is now **drawn on the plakat map** as a labelled marker, and starts
  appearing in the scene briefs of the places near it. A landmark with no position
  stays a gazetteer entry the fact-checker knows by name, exactly as before.

## A wider view from the desk (WORLD-14)

- **The scene brief sees more of its neighbourhood.** `realworld scene` now reports
  the nearest *named feature* — a Place, a declared landmark, or a named water
  (river source / mouth) — with its distance and bearing, not just the nearest
  Place.

## Correctness

- **BUG-14** — duplicate declared nation names made relation binding ambiguous (a
  `with:` reference bound to the first match only). The world now warns rather than
  silently guessing.
- **BUG-16** — `row_to_latitude` (and the lat/lon ↔ grid conversions) disagreed
  with the climate grid by a half-cell (~0.75°). They now use the climate layer's
  cell-centre convention, so a place's latitude matches the climate it actually
  gets.

## Building the World with Inkhaven — the book

- ch. 10 covers `realworld name`; ch. 17 covers positioning declared landmarks;
  ch. 15's scene brief covers the wider proximity; Appendix C documents the
  landmark position keys; plus command-reference rows.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** New command: `realworld name`. `realworld compile
--materialize` now writes three more chapters. New optional `geography.landmarks`
position keys (`lat`/`lon` or `x`/`y`). All additive — existing projects are
unaffected.
