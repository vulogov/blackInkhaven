# Inkhaven 1.6.14 — Index & Argument

_More for the nonfiction desk: a real back-of-book index, a cite picker that surfaces
the source your claim needs, and a first look at the shape of your argument. No new
runtime crates._

## The back-of-book index (INDEX-1)

Inkhaven had a word-frequency concordance but no real index. Now `inkhaven index`
builds one from terms you already curate — every canonical **Glossary** term (its
synonyms become _see_-references) plus any extra names or topics under
`docs.index.terms`. It finds where each appears and writes an alphabetised index with
chapter references, as **Markdown**, **Typst**, or **JSON**.

On the web, `docs.html.include.index` folds an **Index** page into the site where every
entry is a real anchor link to the section it names — a precise, clickable index.

## The citation bridge

The `Ctrl+V @` cite picker used to list every source in key order. Now it **ranks by the
paragraph you're writing** — scoring each source against your prose and floating the
most relevant to the top, marked with a ★ — so the citation your claim needs is usually
the first one you see. Deterministic; no model call.

## A first look at the argument (ARG-1)

`inkhaven argue` reads a chapter and writes back its **central claims and the support
each rests on** — a citation, a line of reasoning, or nothing — and flags the two
cheapest weak joints: a load-bearing claim the text backs with **nothing**, and a
**citation that supports no claim** at all. It quotes your own sentences rather than
inventing an argument on your behalf (a claim it can't find in the chapter is dropped).
Deliberately small — an outline, not a diagram — but often enough to show the one place
your case rests on air. AI-driven; exits non-zero when a gap is found.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** New commands `inkhaven index` and `inkhaven argue`; new config
`docs.index` and `docs.html.include.index`; the `Ctrl+V @` picker is now context-ranked.
Everything is additive — existing projects are unaffected. Test suite → 2370.
