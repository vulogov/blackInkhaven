# Inkhaven 3.1.0 — BONDS: Are the Bonds Earned?

_Inkhaven watches where a character is, whether they exist yet, whether the world holds
together, how their arc bends, whose head we're in, and — since 2.6 — what they know.
None of it watches the thing a reader feels most: how two characters **relate**, and
whether the page earns it._

The flagship of 3.1.0 is **BONDS** — relationship continuity, and the first feature of
the post-3.0 thaw. Its thesis:

> **BONDS is KEN's sibling.** Where KEN checks *knowledge* (who could know what, when),
> BONDS checks *bonds* (how two characters relate, and whether the prose backs it up). A
> declared friendship that never shares a scene, an ally who turns enemy with no turn on
> the page, a marriage that vanishes for ten chapters — the invisible relationship
> plot-holes.

It is **deterministic and free** at the core, **advisory** (it never edits prose), and
it produces a finding a generic AI structurally cannot: judging whether a relationship
is earned across a whole book needs the timeline, the event-participant lists, the
character bible, and scene POV that only inkhaven maintains. It adds **no new runtime
crates**.

## The bond — declared, then checked

BONDS never guesses how characters feel. You **declare**; inkhaven **derives** whether
the page earns it:

- **Declared** — a `rel:<kind>:<A>:<B>` paragraph tag (`rel:ally:mara:kell`,
  `rel:enemy:mara:sella`). `<kind>` is your own vocabulary; the pair is canonical; a
  later differently-kinded tag for the same pair is a **transition**.
- **Derived co-presence** — which characters share each scene, for free: the union of
  the scene's POV, any roster character named in the prose (Unicode-aware), and the
  participants of any timeline event linked into the scene. A generous union — a false
  "present" only makes BONDS quieter, the safe bias for an advisory reader.

The mismatch between the two is the finding.

## What it catches

- **`unwritten_bond`** (Notice) — a declared pair shares fewer than `min_co_presence`
  scenes: asserted, not dramatised.
- **`unearned_shift`** (**Break**) — a declared bond changes state with no shared scene
  in the transition window to turn it: the relationship plot-hole.
- **`dropped_bond`** (Notice) — an established bond goes dormant longer than
  `dormancy_window` chapters, then resurfaces.
- **`implied_cooling`** *(opt-in `--deep`)* — a relationship that warms or cools on the
  page with no `rel:` tag marking it — the subtle, undeclared drift, on a cost-capped
  LLM pass.

## The surfaces

```
inkhaven bonds          # the deterministic check (non-zero exit on an unearned_shift — a CI gate)
inkhaven bonds --json   # the findings as JSON
inkhaven bonds --deep   # + the opt-in, cost-capped implied_cooling pass
```

- **`Ctrl+V Shift+O`** opens the relationship dashboard (findings grouped by kind, Enter
  jumps to the paragraph).
- Bond findings ride the **`Ctrl+V Shift+R`** Editorial Pass (a `bonds` source):
  `unearned_shift` routes to a guided **Decision** (add the scene, or soften the
  declaration); `unwritten_bond` / `dropped_bond` stay advisory **Briefs**.
- `ink.bonds.{ties, findings, check}` — read-only Bund (`check.clean` = no hard break;
  the `--deep` pass is not exposed).

## Cost — a design invariant

The core is a forward walk + set membership + mention matching — **no model, ≈ $0 at any
book size**, scaling with declared bonds, not pages. It is **self-gating**: no `rel:`
tags → no work. The only LLM touchpoint is the opt-in `--deep` pass, cost-capped on the
world fact-checker's rail. There is deliberately no "judge every relationship"
whole-book pass.

## Multilingual & config

BONDS inherits SENTINEL's and KEN's Unicode mention matching; co-presence draws on
timeline participants and POV, which are language-agnostic. New this release: a small
`bonds` config block — `enabled` (the review-pass switch), `min_co_presence` (default
2), and `dormancy_window` (default 6).

## Platform note

Supported release targets remain **Linux** (`x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`) and **macOS**
(`aarch64-apple-darwin`). Windows stays deferred (the `ort-sys` ONNX bindings ship no
windows-gnu prebuilt).
