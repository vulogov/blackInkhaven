# Inkhaven 3.14.0 — Canon at Hand

_The Canon Ledger (3.11–3.13) recorded the decisions behind the fiction, grew its
own dependency graph, and learned to tend it — all from a dashboard, a CLI, and a
Bund surface *off to the side* of writing. 3.14.0 brings the ledger **into the
writing surface**: the paragraphs that established decisions are marked where you
draft, the decisions themselves follow the cursor in a pane of their own, and
"chat with your book" answers from what you *decided*, not only from the prose.
No new store, no new dependency; deterministic except the one existing,
cost-capped AI path._

Full guide: [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md).

---

## The decision-source glyph (CU1-P1)

In the Tree and the Outline, a paragraph that *established* a canon decision
carries a **`◈`** pip. Open one and the status line says how much rests on it —
*"canon: this paragraph sources 2 decision(s) · 3 rest on them · Ctrl+B \* →
Canon"* — so the blast radius is visible the moment you are about to edit the
scene it belongs to. Computed once from the ledger's live decisions (throttled
like the other tree badges); read-only.

## The Canon pane (CU1-P3)

`Ctrl+B Tab` now cycles the right region through **Output · AI · Thoughts ·
Canon**. The Canon pane follows the *open paragraph* and lists the decisions it
sources, live as you move between paragraphs: `[kind] «commitment» gist`, how
many decisions rest on each, and the nearest grounds it rests on (`¶` marks a
ground with a source paragraph). A paragraph change refreshes at once; a slow
tick picks up a harvest-on-save or a dashboard grounding while the pane shows.

| Key | Action |
| --- | ------ |
| `↑`/`↓` (`k`/`j`), `g`/`G` | move between the paragraph's decisions |
| `Enter` | jump to the source paragraph of the first ground — the reading path *upstream* |
| `h` | the decision's history (grounds + commitment trajectory) into Thoughts |
| `*` | the whole-ledger dashboard, cursor already on this decision (`g` grounds it there) |
| `r` · `Esc` | refresh · back to the editor (or leave fullscreen) |

It is a cycled *mode*, not a split: `Ctrl+Z f` fullscreens it and the choice is
remembered across restarts like the other panes. Read-first — grounding stays on
the dashboard and the CLI.

## Grounded chat (CU1-P2)

The AI pane's **Book** scope grounds each conversation on the ledger as well as
the prose. After the passage retrieval, the decisions behind the hits are packed
closure-complete to `canon.context_budget` (the CL-P6 pack the CLI had and the
editor never called) and follow the passages into the prompt, framed in the
project language (en/ru/es/fr/de) as *the author's decisions, not inferences* —
with the instruction to say so when a passage and a decision disagree.

- **`canon.ground_ai`** (default `true`) seeds it; **`*`** in the AI pane toggles
  it per session (off drops the cached pack).
- Transparency: a **`◈ N canon`** cue in the AI title; the `p` section lists the
  packed decisions (kind, commitment, gist, location, tokens used/budget).
- An empty ledger or a pack failure adds nothing to the prompt. Advisory, local.

## Also in this release

- **Canon dashboard graph view** — `t` toggles the reader-hub Canon dashboard
  between the flat list and the grounds DAG (`canon graph` in the editor);
  Enter-jump, `g` ground and `h` history work on either view.
- **Crash hook, class-wide** — a panic on a background thread while the TUI is
  live no longer tears down the terminal: the hook is thread-aware (fail-safe
  when the main thread is unknown), so the render loop survives a contained
  task panic; the F1 help search is single-flight and panic-isolated.
- **Worldbuilder + `realworld` correctness, two passes** (three read-only audits
  and a 35-world edge-value probe; no panics were reachable — the findings were
  wrong results and silent acceptance):
  - The **interview** and the shaping commands wrote a `world.hjson` the schema
    could not load (`/star`, `/tilt` to stray keys, `/moon` with `period`,
    `/nation` with non-schema fields and no capital; `/write` folded onto `{}`
    and never validated). Now: a **World** stage asks the name first; answers
    in the prompt's own words (`orange` → K with a class-typical luminosity);
    `/moon <name…> [period_days]`; `/nation <name…> [x y]` with `capital`
    **optional** (unpinned nations seat at the largest unclaimed settlement,
    pinned first); every delta is **validated against the schema when accepted**
    (refusals name the delta; a scalar the schema wants as text is retried as
    text; a typo path is refused); `/write` folds onto the starter scaffold on a
    fresh project, validates, keeps the previous file at
    `.inkhaven/world.hjson.bak`, and never replaces a file that exists but does
    not parse; `/undo` drops a whole delta; corrupt session files are set aside.
  - **`realworld validate` lints the definition's own domain** (0-month calendar,
    dark star, open orbit, tilt outside 0..180, `sea_level` outside 0..1, unknown
    `stance` / `rotation_direction` / `mountain_orogeny` spellings, a relation
    with an undeclared nation, a seed that is neither hex nor decimal) before
    compiling the layers — advisory; the worldbuilder score includes them.
  - Fixed: `realworld calendar` emitted the unit stack one level down (12-day
    years when adopted); `compile_history` ignored declared nations; events at
    year 0 vanished from every listing; negative `--lat` / `--from-x` rejected;
    culture-style settlement names collided; 8-bit DEM `sea_level_pixel_value`
    compared against 16-bit samples; realm membership, `realworld name` and the
    scene brief used two different distance metrics; `realworld new` wrote the
    world name unescaped; `critique --write-notes` stacked duplicate Notes;
    `gazetteer --output world.hjson` could clobber the definition; the Ctrl+B W
    overview / scene brief / `/calc` fallback showed the procedural twin of a DEM
    world; materialize now stops at the first failing layer and skips unchanged
    leaves; the HJSON depth guard could be blinded by an apostrophe in a
    quoteless value; `calc.world.*` Bund words read the store but counted as pure
    (now `store_read`).
- **Books:** *Building the World with Inkhaven* follows every worldbuilder /
  `realworld` change (interview stages, command forms, accept-time checks, the
  definition lints, optional capitals, DEM pixel depth, required fields);
  Manual ch19 and Know-Your-Book ch8 gain the glyph, the pane, and grounded chat.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to migrate. Canon grounding of Book-scope chat is on by default and costs
nothing on an empty ledger; set `canon.ground_ai: false` (or press `*` in the AI
pane) to ground on prose only. A `world.hjson` corrupted by the old interview is
now *refused* by `/write` rather than overwritten — delete it and re-run the
interview, or `realworld new --force`.
