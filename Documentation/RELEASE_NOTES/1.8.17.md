# Inkhaven 1.8.17 — Poetry with Inkhaven (POEM PO-P11 book · PO-P12 editor)

_The poetry layer gets its companion book and its first proper editor affordance. A full
standalone book, **Poetry with Inkhaven**, teaches the whole toolset for both writing verse
and writing about it; and you can now **declare a form at the desk** instead of hand-editing
HJSON. **No new runtime crates.**_

## The companion book — _Poetry with Inkhaven_

A new 59-page companion book joins _Developing a Constructed Language_ and _Linguistic
Research with Inkhaven_ under [`Book/POETRY/`](../../Book/POETRY/) — compile with
`typst compile Book/POETRY/POETRY.typ`. It serves **two readers at once**: the poet who wants
their own lines measured, and the critic who wants to interrogate someone else's — which is
why the title drops "writing." Eight chapters, from a single syllable to a whole corpus:

- **The line** — the `para:verse-*` family and the forms library; syllabification and stress
  across five languages (honest about English's rough edges, sharp in Russian).
- **Measure** — scansion and the three-mark scan (`/ × ·`); rhyme quality, type, and the
  per-language normalisation (German devoicing, French mute-e, Russian akanye).
- **The whole poem** — the Inner Poet's fast and slow tracks; form completion and the volta.
- **Beyond the original** — the translator's trilemma (form/meaning/sound, with meaning left
  to judgement); scripting the engines in Bund and gating a manuscript in CI.

Every example is real output from the shipped commands, and every honest limitation (English
metre and eye-rhyme read from spelling alone) is stated plainly rather than hidden.

## Declare a form in the editor (PO-P12)

Until now, the `poem:` block that every measuring tool reads had to be hand-written as an
HJSON sidecar. No longer. In the Inner Poet overview (`Ctrl+B J → P`), press **`D`** to open a
**form picker**; choose one of the eighteen built-in forms and Inkhaven writes its `poem:`
block — localised to the project language — as a sidecar beside the open stanza, exactly where
the Inner Poet cascades to find it. The fast track (`F`), `status`, and the slow track (`E`)
then have a target to measure against.

- Writes (or rewrites, if one already exists) the sidecar; never duplicates.
- **Generates no verse** — it writes a *form declaration*, not a poem. The observe-never-write
  rule holds.
- A new guard test round-trips **every** built-in form in **every** language through
  `to_poem_block` → `from_hjson`, so the editor can never write a block the reader can't parse.

## Also: the `poetry` genre

The general prose readers (Inner Socrates) now recognise `genre: "poetry"` (aliases `verse`,
`poem`, `poetic`), so line breaks, fragments, and compression read as the medium rather than
as errors — metre and rhyme stay the Inner Poet's job.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** The book is Typst-only (bundled fonts, bare `typst compile`). The
editor feature reuses the shipped node-creation and content-write primitives. Warning-free
(binary and tests). Test suite → 2654.
