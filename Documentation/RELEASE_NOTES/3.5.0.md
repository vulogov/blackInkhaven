# Inkhaven 3.5.0 — Finished Imports, One Reader Hub, a Deeper KEN

_A **widen-and-polish** release: no new capability. It finishes the export/import
fidelity work, gives the now-large reader family a single front door, and turns
KEN from an error-checker into a queryable knowledge model._

Three tracks. Deterministic, no new runtime crates.

## Track I · Export / import, finished

The three fidelity items the 3.4.0 audit deferred:

- **Scrivener non-ASCII import.** A Cyrillic/accented `.rtf` used to force a
  plain-text fallback that dropped **all** bold/italic/heading markup. Now the
  structured parser protects each non-ASCII character behind an ASCII placeholder
  through the (ASCII-only) upstream lexer and restores it in the output — so
  Russian/French Scrivener docs keep both their text **and** their structure.
- **EPUB-import footnote round-trip.** `inkhaven epub` exports footnotes as a
  noteref + a collected `<aside>`, and the importer read neither — so every
  footnote was lost. Now an inline `<a epub:type="noteref">` is rebuilt as
  `#footnote[body]` from its collected aside, and the footnotes section is
  suppressed. A true `typst → xhtml → typst` round-trip.
- **Epub toy retired.** All EPUB export — CLI, the Bund `ink.export.epub` word,
  the TUI batch builder — now goes through the rich, multi-chapter builder
  (`build_epub_bytes` returns the in-memory bytes the Bund/TUI callers need);
  `src/export/epub.rs` is deleted.

## Track H · The reader hub

With a dozen readers, their dashboards were scattered across a dozen chords.

- **`Ctrl+B *`** opens the **reader hub** — a menu of every reader dashboard
  (Knowledge, Bonds, Continuity, Read-through, Cast, Character arc, Myth,
  Chronicle, Story bible), each with its **live finding-count** (from the unified
  worklist, for the deterministic readers). Enter opens the selected reader's
  dashboard.
- **`inkhaven read`** — the CLI companion: a glanceable *state of the manuscript*
  (finding-counts grouped by reader + a health line). The *overview* to
  `inkhaven edit`'s full worklist.

## Track D · A deeper KEN

KEN computed the whole grants model — who could know what, when — but only ever
showed the *errors*. Now it surfaces the model:

- **`inkhaven knowledge --ledger`** (`--json`) — the grants grouped by character,
  each topic with the chapter it becomes knowable and how (`presence` from a
  timeline event, or `declared` via a tag).
- **`l`** in the `Ctrl+B Shift+Z` dashboard toggles between the findings and the
  ledger; Enter on a ledger row jumps to where the character learns the topic.

No new finding-noise (the ledger is a *view* of `build_grants`, not a new check) —
KEN stays silent where it can't ground.

## Deferred as follow-ups

The audit-flagged, lower-value items left for later: blockquote/table
reconstruction on third-party EPUB import (inkhaven's own epub emits neither), and
alias resolution (still gated on its architectural decision).

## Platform note

Supported release targets remain **Linux** (`x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`) and
**macOS** (`aarch64-apple-darwin`). Windows stays deferred (the `ort-sys` ONNX
bindings ship no windows-gnu prebuilt).
