# Inkhaven 1.8.12 — The Inner Poet Listens (POEM-3, PO-P6 slow track)

_The Inner Poet gains its LLM slow track. `Ctrl+B J → P` now opens a small overview: **F**
fast-scans the stanza's metre and rhyme (to the Output pane), and **E** engages the AI to
observe what the verse is *doing* — enjambment, sound texture, caesura, the turn — in the
Thoughts pane. Observations, never prescriptions; it never rewrites. **No new runtime
crates.**_

## Fast and slow, in one place

`Ctrl+B J → P` opens the Inner Poet overview:

- **`F` — fast track** (deterministic, 1.8.11): each line's metre and the stanza's rhyme
  scheme checked against the declared `poem:` form → `♪` findings in the Output pane.
- **`E` — slow track** (new): an LLM reads the stanza and shares a few observations in the
  Thoughts pane — whether the line breaks are enjambed to effect or cut across the syntax,
  the alliterative and assonant texture, where the caesura falls, and — for a sonnet — whether
  a real **volta** is present at the expected position (after line 8 Petrarchan, line 12
  Shakespearean). The volta question is asked *only* for sonnet forms.

The voice is the Inner Editor's — "I notice…", "you might consider…", never "should". The
Inner Poet **never rewrites** a line or proposes a replacement word; silence on a clean
stanza is correct.

## What's next

The remaining Inner-Poet pieces — a session store (`inner_poet.db`), the live status-bar

detection (PO-P7) and the translation workspace (PO-P8) follow.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** A new `inner_poet::slow` module (prompt + LLM call reusing the
shared blocking-completion path), a `BgJobKind::PoetSlow` background job, and a small Inner
Poet overview modal. Warning-free (binary and tests). Test suite → 2643.
