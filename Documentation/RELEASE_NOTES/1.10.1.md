# Inkhaven 1.10.1 — The Cartographer Draws More

_1.10.0 gave the worldbuilder a map editor for the human layer — towns, landmarks,
rivers, regions, and the map-check. 1.10.1 finishes it: **roads**, **terrain sculpting**,
**mouse support**, and a full companion-book chapter. Every mark is still an ordinary
`world.hjson` edit._

## Roads

Press `o` in the Map pane's edit mode and connect two named landmarks — move to the
first and `Enter`, to the second and `Enter`. The road lands in `geography.roads[]` and,
because it references landmarks the map already knows, draws between them (`=`) both in
the worldbuilder and on the **plakat raster** — declared roads now emit as `RoadSpec`s
alongside the trade-derived ones.

## Terrain sculpting

Press `+` to raise the land under a soft brush and `-` to lower it (`,`/`.` size it). The
map re-shades live as you sculpt — `~` sea, `.` lowland, `^` hills, `A` peaks — so you
can raise a range or carve a bay and watch it appear. `/terrain` writes the sculpted
heightmap as a grayscale **DEM** under `assets/maps/` and points `geology.dem` at it; a
`realworld compile` (DEM-aware) then rebuilds the whole world — climate, rivers,
settlements — from your land.

_Known follow-up: the worldbuilder's own `/compile` previews the sculpt and writes the
DEM but stays procedural; the terrain takes full effect through `realworld compile`. A
DEM-aware worldbuilder compile is planned._

## Mouse

If your terminal supports it, a left-click in the Map pane focuses it, enters edit mode,
and drops the cursor on the clicked cell — click to point, then place with the single
keys. Mouse capture is enabled only for the worldbuilder, so the research and linguistic
companions keep their native selection and scroll.

## Documentation

*Building the World with Inkhaven* gains **Chapter 21, "The Map Editor,"** with five
faithful terminal screens (the cursor, placing a town, drawing a river, sculpting a
mountain range, and `/mapcheck` flagging a town in the sea). The `WORLDBUILDING.md` and
`KEYBINDING.md` references carry the full map-editor key and command tables.

## Compatibility

No new dependencies. All changes are additive and backward-compatible: `geography.roads[]`
is new, `geology.dem` was already supported by the compiler, and mouse capture is scoped
to the worldbuilder. Compiles warning-free. Test suite → 2723.
