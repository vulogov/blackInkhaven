# Inkhaven 1.8.30 — The research book learns RESRCH-6

_The companion book *Grounding Your Book in Fact* catches up with the autonomous research
tools, and gains faithful terminal screens throughout. Documentation only. **No code changes.**_

## A new chapter — "Research That Researches Itself"

`Book/RESEARCH` gains Chapter 15, teaching the RESRCH-6 features shipped across 1.8.26–1.8.29
for authors with no prior knowledge of them:

- **`--agentic "<topic>"`** — how autonomous research plans its own sub-questions, gathers
  evidence, and *emits its findings as untrusted Facts into the Facts book* (never a standalone
  article); the gap-driven iterate loop; the bounded stop condition; the in-loop contradiction
  gate; and the `research.agentic` switch (on by default, how to turn it off).
- **`--snowball "<seed>"`** — following a paper's citations backward and forward on OpenAlex to
  map its neighborhood, and why it surfaces rather than swamps.

The chapter keeps the book's two-audience voice and its "the machine widens your reach, you
decide what's true" discipline.

## Terminal screens

Ported the `screen()` helper from the poetry companion — a faithful monospace rendering of a
terminal, self-contained and warning-free. It's now used wherever a TUI or CLI screen helps the
reader, including three new ones:

- the two-pane Research Assistant (Facts tree ∥ conversation) in Chapter 2,
- the confirmation gate in Chapter 3,
- an agentic run in progress, its report, and a snowball citation neighborhood in Chapter 15.

## Dependencies & compatibility

`typst compile`. Test suite unchanged → 2665.
