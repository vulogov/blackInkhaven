# Inkhaven 1.6.18 — Scripture & Loci

_Public-domain scripture as a research source, citation down to the passage, and
SCHOLAR brought into the manuscript editor — the primary-source apparatus a work
of theology or philosophy actually needs. No new runtime crates._

## Scripture as a research source

Three keyless, public-domain adapters bring scripture into the Research Assistant,
verse by verse:

- `/bible <book> <chapter>` — a Bible passage (bolls.life)
- `/quran <surah>` — a surah of the Qur'an (api.alquran.cloud)
- `/bookofmormon <book> <chapter>` — a Book of Mormon passage (the public-domain
  1830 text)

Each ingests the passage **verse-structured** (every chunk carries its verse
references), and auto-cites the work under a **stable key** — `bible`, `quran`,
`book-of-mormon` — so every passage you ever gather from a work files under one
source. Translation follows the project `language`: English draws the World English
Bible and Sahih International, Russian the Synodal Bible and Kuliev, and French,
German, and Spanish each a public-domain translation of their own; override per work
with `research.scripture.bible_translation` / `quran_translation` (set
`quran-uthmani` for the Arabic original). CLI twins: `inkhaven research --bible /
--quran / --bookofmormon`.

## Primary-source loci and the Index Locorum

A theologian cites a passage, not a work. `@key[locus]` — `@bible[John 3:16]`,
`@kant[A51/B75]`, `@quran[2:255]` — is now first-class. It is native Typst (the
bracket is the citation supplement), so the on-disk prose is unchanged; Inkhaven's
addition is to **harvest** every locus into an **Index Locorum**:

```
inkhaven index-locorum                    # Markdown (also --format typst|json)
```

It walks the manuscript, resolves each key to its source title from the Sources
book, and renders the apparatus grouped by source, loci sorted naturally
(`3:2` before `3:16`), each tagged with the chapter it was cited in. Set
`sources.index_locorum: true` and `inkhaven build` folds it into the finished book
after the bibliography.

## SCHOLAR P3 — the persistent report

`/contradict`, `/converge`, and `/relate` used to render their findings and drop
them. Now they merge into a persistent, **topic-clustered report** at
`.inkhaven/scholar_report.json`, accumulating across sessions and scan kinds.
`/report` (+ `inkhaven research --report`) renders it grouped by topic — every
contradiction, convergence, and relation you've found — and **flags staleness** when
the Facts corpus has changed since the findings were gathered, via an
order-independent hash of the fact texts. Headings localize to en/ru/fr/de/es.

## SCHOLAR P4 — confront the open paragraph

`Ctrl+V ?` in the manuscript editor confronts the open paragraph against the research
corpus — the manuscript-side twin of `/relate`. It retrieves the nearest facts and
source passages, judges each against the prose with the graded relation engine, and
emits **anchored** findings (the ⚔ glyph) to the Output pane: an *against* relation
(contradicts / tension) as a warning, a *supporting* one (agrees / qualifies) as
citable information, silent sources dropped. Grounding stops being a thing you did
before writing and becomes a reader over your shoulder as you write.

## A companion book — Theology and Philosophy with Inkhaven

Two new companion volumes under `Book/THEOLOGY_AND_PHILOSOPHY/` demonstrate the whole
track on one worked question — Kant's transcendental idealism and Latter-day Saint
eternal progression. The **manual** runs the research loop end to end (framing,
scripture gathering, SCHOLAR, the Inner Theologian, confront, loci, the Index
Locorum, production); the **essay** is the scholarly outcome that process produces,
with real `@key[locus]` citations, a bibliography, and a generated Index Locorum.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** New commands `/bible`, `/quran`, `/bookofmormon`,
`/report`, and `inkhaven index-locorum`; new editor chord `Ctrl+V ?`; new config
`research.scripture` and `sources.index_locorum`. Everything is additive — existing
projects are unaffected, and the new apparatus is opt-in. Test suite → 2440.
