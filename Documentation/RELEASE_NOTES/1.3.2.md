## Headline — diagnose the structure, don't organize cards

Authors don't struggle to organize (corkboards are everywhere) — they
struggle to **diagnose** ("something's wrong with my middle but I can't
see it"). 1.3.2 turns that into a specific, located, objective finding.

Structure is the **third axis** alongside the existing Timeline (*when*)
and Threads (*whether arcs pay off*): acts, beats, turning points, the
shape of the rise and fall. The Board references threads/timeline — it
never re-implements them.

- **Frameworks + model** — a new `Planning` system book; beats as
  HJSON-fronted paragraphs (the Threads pattern); built-in beat sheets
  (three-act, Save the Cat, Story Circle, Hero's Journey, Seven-Point).
- **Coverage + pacing (deterministic)** — the sleeper feature: which beats
  are gaps, position drift (midpoint at 0.65 vs target 0.50), and
  act-proportion pacing ("Act 2 is 30% of your words") — objective, no AI,
  works on any draft instantly.
- **Structure-outline view** — a TUI surface (not a 2-D corkboard): beats
  down the page with a position/coverage bar showing drift + gaps at a
  glance.
- **AI analyze** — over the 1.3.1 book digest + the chosen framework, map
  the draft to the beats and name the sag.
- **Insight overlays** — the `tension scan` curve vs the framework's
  expected shape; beats linked to the threads/timeline they advance.
