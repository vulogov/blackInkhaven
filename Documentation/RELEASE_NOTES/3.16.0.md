# Inkhaven 3.16.0 — The Ledger Reads Back

_Through 3.15 the Canon Ledger only answered: what breaks if I cut this, why is
this canon, how settled is it. 3.16.0 makes it speak first. The ledger now reads
itself against the manuscript and reports where the two have come apart — as a
reader, alongside SENTINEL, KEN and BONDS, in the same worklist, history and
dashboards. Deterministic and free at its core; one opt-in model pass for the
case counting cannot see. No new store, no new dependency; warning-free._

## `canon read` — the four free findings

```
inkhaven canon read [<scope>] [--json] [--strict] [--deep] [--max-cost N]
```

| Finding | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Drifted from its source** | The paragraph that established a decision no longer mentions any of it (stemmed, in the project language; a `rel:`-tagged decision is carried by its tag). |
| **Orphaned** | That paragraph no longer exists. |
| **Built on sand** | Committed above something it rests on. |
| **Unsettled foundation** | Two or more decisions rest on one still unmarked or floated — reported once you have committed something. |

`<scope>` narrows the report to a chapter or paragraph; `--json` is for scripts;
`--strict` exits non-zero when there is anything to report. `canon check` is
unchanged.

## The contradiction pass — opt-in

`canon read --deep`, or **`D`** on the Canon dashboard, asks a model **once**
whether any passage of the book says the *opposite* of a decision you marked
committed or canonical. Passages are retrieved locally; everything goes in one
prompt fitted to the budget (`--max-cost`, or `canon.deep_budget` in the editor,
both 8000 tokens by default) — decisions that do not fit are reported as
unchecked, the run is never refused. The model must cite the decision and the
passage; a citation it was not offered is dropped.

The answer is stored, so every later read, the Editorial Pass, CHRONICLE and the
dashboard show it without another call — and a stored finding **expires on its
own** when the passage changes or the decision is no longer committed.

## Where the findings appear

- **Canon dashboard** — findings listed first; `Enter` opens the paragraph
  involved, `c` / `g` / `h` act on the decision.
- **Canon pane** — a marker under the decision (`⚠ drifted`, `⚠ on sand`, …).
- **Reader hub** — Canon shows its count beside the other readers.
- **Editorial Pass / `inkhaven revise`** — under the source `canon`. *Drifted* and
  *contradicted* are **Decisions** (the scene or the ledger — you say which); the
  rest are **Briefs** (you reconcile the ledger; no prose is touched).
- **CHRONICLE** — counted in every milestone, so the trend shows whether the
  ledger and the book are converging.
- **Bund** — `ink.canon.read ( -- list )`, read-only.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to migrate. The four free findings appear as soon as you open the Canon
dashboard or run `canon read`; if a long-lived ledger reports many *drifted*
decisions, that is the reader doing its job on prose that moved — each is a
choice, not an error, and can be deferred in the Editorial Pass like any finding.
The contradiction pass never runs unless you ask for it.
