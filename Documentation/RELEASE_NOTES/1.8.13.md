# Inkhaven 1.8.13 — Form Completion (POEM-6, PO-P7)

_Know where a poem stands against its form. `inkhaven poetry status` reports a completion
ratio and flags the structural components a form requires but the text is missing or
breaking — a villanelle's refrain schedule, a pantoum's carried lines, a ghazal's signature.
**No new runtime crates.**_

## Completion and missing components

```sh
inkhaven poetry status --form villanelle --text "…"
#   ♩ villanelle · 6/19 · drafting
#     ⚠ Villanelle: line 6 should repeat the first refrain "…"
```

`poetry status` reports the line ratio against the form's expected total (14 for a sonnet,
19 for a villanelle, 3 for a haiku, or `stanzas × lines_per_stanza`; open forms just count
lines) and whether it's `drafting` or `complete`. Then it runs the form-specific checks:

- **Villanelle** — the two refrains (lines 1 and 3) must recur on schedule (lines 6/12/18 and
  9/15/19); a refrain line that drifts is flagged.
- **Pantoum** — lines 2 and 4 of each quatrain must return as lines 1 and 3 of the next.
- **Ghazal** — the closing couplet (maqta) should name the poet (the declared
  `signature_word`).
- **Sonnet** — fourteen lines; an over-length one is flagged.

All deterministic and offline — it measures, never rewrites. The volta observation itself
lives in the Inner Poet's slow track (1.8.12), which asks about the turn for sonnet forms.
