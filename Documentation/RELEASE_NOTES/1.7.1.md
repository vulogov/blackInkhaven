# Inkhaven 1.7.1 — The Phonologist's Toolkit

_The 1.7 linguistic layer grows its first analysis family: a distinctive-feature
matrix, and two instruments that read a language's sound system through it —
minimal pairs and inventory naturalness. Deterministic, corpus-free, and
surfaced in both the CLI and the Linguistic companion. No new runtime crates._

## A distinctive-feature matrix

Under the new analyses sits a curated, PHOIBLE-aligned lookup from IPA segments to
their **distinctive features** — place, manner, voicing, height, backness,
rounding and more, for ~85 common segments. It is a separate table keyed by IPA
(the phoneme model itself is unchanged), and it answers the question every
phonological analysis needs: *how do these two sounds differ?*

## Minimal pairs — the functional load of your contrasts

`inkhaven language pairs <lang>` finds every **minimal pair** in the dictionary —
two words identical but for a single segment, the classic evidence that the two
sounds contrast — and, through the feature matrix, reports the one feature each
pair turns on:

```
functional load (single-feature contrasts):
    voice        ████████████ 14
    place        ██████ 7
    nasal        ███ 3
```

So you can see, at a glance, which contrasts your language leans on and which are
barely used — the *functional load* of every distinction you've drawn.

## Naturalness — your inventory against the baseline

`inkhaven language naturalness <lang>` judges the phoneme inventory against
cross-linguistic tendencies: **voicing-pair symmetry** (does every voiceless
obstruent have a voiced counterpart, or is there a `/k/`-without-`/ɡ/` gap?),
**place-series coverage**, presence of the **near-universal segments** (`/m a i
n t k/`…), and inventory size — folded into a transparent 0–1 score. A gap is a
**flag, not an error**: many natural languages lack `/p/` or `/ɡ/`; the point is
to show you where your inventory is ordinary and where it is marked.

## In the companion

Both read straight from the language sub-book you're in. The Linguistic
companion's right-pane **Phonology** view now shows the quantitative metrics
*and* the naturalness summary together, and a new **Minimal pairs** view (`p`)
lists the contrasts. `Tab` cycles them alongside Chat, Preview and Universals.

## Dependencies & compatibility

**No new runtime crates.** Everything is additive — two new `language`
subcommands (`pairs`, `naturalness`) and a bundled feature-data file; existing
projects and languages are unchanged. Warning-free (binary and tests). Test suite
→ 2495.
